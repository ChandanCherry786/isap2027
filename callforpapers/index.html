Call for Papers

Conference theme: Trustworthy Intelligence for Resilient and Autonomous Power Systems

Paper submission opens on December 15, 2026, through Microsoft CMT. The full paper submission deadline is February 28, 2027.

Invitation

Michigan State University is proud to host the International Conference on Intelligent Systems Applications to Power Systems (ISAP 2027). The conference invites researchers, practitioners, and students to submit original contributions on intelligent systems for electric power and energy systems.

For more than three decades, ISAP has brought the power systems and intelligent systems communities together to discuss the operation, control, planning, and maintenance of interconnected and isolated grids. The program will include tutorials, workshops, plenary sessions, technical paper sessions, and panel discussions. Students are encouraged to present, and the organizers are pursuing reduced student registration and travel support.

Technical tracks

Contributions are sought on intelligent systems approaches of every kind for the planning, operation, control, and protection of power and energy systems, from generation to grid-edge resources and markets. The topics below illustrate each track and are not exhaustive.

  1. Foundation Models and Generative AI for Power and Energy Systems

    • Large language models and natural-language interfaces for grid operation
    • Time-series foundation models and generative scenario models
    • LLM assistants for operators, planners, and protection engineers

  2. Physics-Informed and Trustworthy AI for Power System Operation and Planning

    • Physics-informed learning for state estimation, power flow, and dynamics
    • Explainable, verifiable, and uncertainty-aware machine learning
    • Safe learning-based control that respects network constraints

  3. Intelligent Control and Optimization of DERs, EVs, and Grid-Edge Resources

    • Coordination of distributed energy resources, storage, and virtual power plants
    • Electric vehicle charging and vehicle-to-grid services
    • Reinforcement learning and distributed optimization for microgrids

  4. AI for Power System Resilience, Reliability, and Extreme Events

    • Impact prediction for extreme weather, wildfire, and cascading outages
    • Outage management, service restoration, and resource adequacy
    • Fault diagnosis, condition monitoring, and asset health

  5. Digital Twins and Autonomous Agents for Future Power Systems

    • Real-time digital twins and hardware-in-the-loop validation
    • Agentic AI and multi-agent systems for autonomous grid operation
    • Self-healing networks and decision support for control rooms

  6. Large Loads, AI Data Centers, and Grid Integration

    • Forecasting and planning for data-center and compute-load growth
    • Flexibility, ride-through, and dynamics of large electronic loads
    • Protection and interconnection requirements for large loads

  7. Cyber-Physical Security and Privacy-Preserving Intelligence

    • Detection of cyberattacks and false data injection
    • Federated and privacy-preserving learning across utilities
    • Secure communication for wide-area monitoring and control

  8. Machine Learning and Data-Driven Methods for Power Systems

    • Supervised, unsupervised, and deep learning on grid data
    • AI-based load, renewable generation, and price forecasting
    • Synchrophasor analytics, market analytics, and big data

Paper submission

  • Full papers of up to six pages in IEEE PES double-column format
  • Submit through Microsoft CMT between December 15, 2026, and February 28, 2027
  • Similarity index below 20% excluding references and below 30% including references
  • Any use of AI-generated content disclosed in the Acknowledgments section
  • Every submission is peer reviewed by the international Technical Program Committee
  • Accepted papers will be published in IEEE Xplore, subject to presentation at the conference
