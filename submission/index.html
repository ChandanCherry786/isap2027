Paper Submission

Instructions for authors preparing a full paper for ISAP 2027.

Paper format

Full papers are limited to six pages in the IEEE PES double-column format. The IEEE conference templates for Word and LaTeX are available from the IEEE template page.

Each paper is submitted to one of the eight technical tracks. Authors should choose the track that best matches the main contribution of the paper. The tracks and their topics are listed in the call for papers.

Submission system

Papers are submitted through Microsoft CMT. Submission opens on December 15, 2026, and closes on February 28, 2027. The link to the ISAP 2027 submission site will be posted on this page when submission opens.

Originality and similarity check

ISAP 2027 accepts original contributions only. Every submission is checked for similarity with published work, and the similarity index must stay within two limits:

Similarity indexLimit
Excluding the reference listBelow 20%
Including the reference listBelow 30%

Submissions above either limit may be rejected without review.

Use of AI tools

Authors must disclose any content generated by artificial intelligence (AI) in the Acknowledgments section of the paper, in line with the IEEE submission policies. This applies to text, figures, images, and code. The disclosure names the AI system, identifies the sections that contain AI-generated content, and briefly explains how the system was used.

AI tools used only for editing and grammar fall outside this requirement, although disclosure is still recommended. AI tools cannot be listed as authors, and the authors remain responsible for the accuracy and originality of the entire paper.

Example: “The authors used ChatGPT (OpenAI) to improve the language of Sections I and V. The authors reviewed the edited text and take full responsibility for the content of this paper.”

Review and decisions

Every submission is peer reviewed by the international Technical Program Committee. Authors are notified of the decision by April 30, 2027. Authors of accepted papers submit a revised paper by June 15, 2027, which takes the reviewers' comments into account.

Publication

Accepted papers will be published in IEEE Xplore, subject to presentation at the conference.
