ISAP 2027 / Program

Program

Four days at the Kellogg Hotel & Conference Center. Keynote speakers, tutorials, workshops, and the detailed session schedule will be announced.

Tentative schedule

  1. September 12Opening day
    Registration, tutorials, and workshops
  2. September 13
    Plenary talks, parallel paper sessions, and panel discussions
  3. September 14
    Plenary talks, parallel paper sessions, and panel discussions
  4. September 15Closing day
    Parallel paper sessions, closing remarks, and adjournment

Session formats

Plenary talks bring the whole conference together around invited speakers. Accepted papers are presented in parallel technical sessions. Panel discussions give researchers, utilities, and industry a shared forum on open questions in the field.

Program updates

Speaker and session announcements are sent to the ISAP 2027 mailing list.
